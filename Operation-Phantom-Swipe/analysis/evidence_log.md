\## Findings



1\. Four fictional card records were present in the simulated

&#x20;  skimmer data.

2\. Device PS-SK-047 was associated with the records.

3\. The device log recorded a simulated transfer.

4\. Messages referred to the captured records and protected storage.

6\. Transaction records showed simulated attempted fraudulent use.

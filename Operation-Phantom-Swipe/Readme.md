\# Operation Phantom Swipe



\## Digital Forensics Investigation Simulation



Operation Phantom Swipe is an academic digital-forensics simulation

involving a fictional ATM skimming device and a suspect mobile device.



\## Objectives



\- Classify simulated cybercrimes

\- Simulate electronic evidence acquisition

\- Maintain chain of custody

\- Generate SHA-256 integrity hashes

\- Extract forensic artefacts

\- Perform controlled password recovery

\- Examine legal and ethical considerations

\- Analyse cross-border investigation challenges
